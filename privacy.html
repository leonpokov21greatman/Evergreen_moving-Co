Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Evergreen Moving Co. ("we", "us") collects on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you fill out our quote form, we receive:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Details about your move, such as your moving date, the towns or ZIP codes you're moving from and to, the type of move, and any notes you add

If you call or email us, we receive the information you share in that conversation.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and give you a quote
  • To plan, schedule, and carry out your move
  • To contact you about your move

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Services that help us

Quote form messages are delivered to our email through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, and are stored in our email account (Gmail, provided by Google). These services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. The font and icons are stored on this website, so loading a page does not contact other companies.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and move records only as long as we need them to serve you, keep business records, or meet legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond as soon as we can.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
