How Our Local Moving Works

Local moves are priced by the hour, based on how many movers you need and how long the move takes. Before you book, we ask about the size of your home, stairs, elevators, and any heavy items, so you know what to expect.

On moving day we protect floors and door frames, wrap and pad your furniture, load the truck carefully, and set everything up in the right rooms at your new place.

What's Included

  • Furniture wrapping and padding
  • Taking apart and putting back together beds and tables
  • Loading, driving, and unloading
  • Floor and door-frame protection
  • Placing furniture where you want it
  • Optional packing and boxes

Tips Before Your Move

Reserve parking

Ask your neighbors or the city about space for the truck close to your door at both homes.

Measure big items

Check that large couches and dressers fit through doors and stairways at the new place.

Pack a first-night box

Keep chargers, medicine, toiletries, and a change of clothes with you.

Local Moving Questions

How are local moves priced?

Local moves are priced by the hour. The total depends on the number of movers, the size of your home, stairs, heavy items, and the distance between homes. Ask for a free quote to get your exact price.

Which areas count as a local move?

Moves within Baltimore and to nearby towns such as Towson, Columbia, Catonsville, Dundalk, Glen Burnie, Ellicott City, and Owings Mills are local moves. Call us if you're not sure.

Can you move just a few items?

Yes. We can move a single room, a few pieces of furniture, or a whole house.
