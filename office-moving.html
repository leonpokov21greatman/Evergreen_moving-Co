How Our Office Moving Works

We start by walking through your current space and your new one, then make a plan for desks, chairs, files, computers, and shared equipment.

Everything is labeled by room or workstation, so on the first day in your new office, every desk is where it belongs.

What's Included

  • A move plan built around your business hours
  • Desk and furniture disassembly and setup
  • Careful moving of files and equipment
  • Labels by room or workstation
  • Evening and weekend moves
  • Removal of packing materials afterwards

Tips Before Your Move

Pick a move lead

Choose one person at your company to be our main contact.

Label everything

Give each person a number or color for their desk, boxes, and equipment.

Back up your data

Back up computers and servers before moving day.

Office Moving Questions

Can you move our office after business hours?

Yes. We can move in the evening or on weekends so your business keeps running.

Do you move computers and equipment?

Yes. We wrap and move computers, monitors, printers, and other equipment. Your IT team should disconnect and reconnect networks and servers.

How long does an office move take?

It depends on the size of your office. A small office can often move in one day.
