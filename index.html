Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire movers in Baltimore?

Local moves are priced by the hour. The price depends on how many movers you need, the size of your home, stairs, heavy items, and the distance between homes. Call 410-324-9898 or request a free quote for your exact price.

How far ahead should I book my move?

Two to four weeks ahead is best, especially at the start or end of the month and during summer. We can sometimes fit in last-minute moves, so call to check.

Do you supply boxes and packing materials?

Yes. We can bring boxes, tape, bubble wrap, and wardrobe boxes, or pack everything for you with our full packing service.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Baltimore, Maryland and serve nearby towns including Towson, Annapolis, Columbia, Ellicott City, Glen Burnie, Catonsville, Dundalk, Owings Mills, and Bel Air. We also handle long-distance moves to other states.

What should I do to get ready for moving day?

Label boxes by room, keep valuables and important papers with you, empty and defrost the fridge, and make sure there is parking close to the door for the truck.
