How Our Specialty Item Moving Works

Before moving day we ask about the item's size, weight, and the path in and out of both homes - including stairs, turns, and doorways - so we bring the right equipment and crew.

Fragile and valuable pieces are wrapped and padded, moved slowly and carefully, and placed exactly where you want them.

What's Included

  • Planning for heavy or awkward items
  • Extra padding and custom wrapping
  • Moving equipment for heavy pieces
  • Careful handling of artwork and mirrors
  • Antique and heirloom furniture
  • Placement in your new home

Tips Before Your Move

Tell us everything

Let us know about stairs, tight turns, and the item's weight when you book.

Take photos

Photograph valuable items before the move for your own records.

Specialty Item Moving Questions

Can you move a piano?

Yes. Tell us the type of piano and the stairs or turns at both homes, and we'll plan the right crew and equipment.

Can you move a gun safe?

Yes. Let us know its size and weight when you ask for a quote.

How do you protect artwork and mirrors?

We wrap and pad them and keep them upright and secure in the truck.
