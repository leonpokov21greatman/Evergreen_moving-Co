How Our Long-Distance Moving Works

Long-distance moves take more planning than local ones. We go over your inventory, pickup and delivery dates, and anything that needs special care, so there are no surprises on moving day.

Your furniture is wrapped and padded, your boxes are loaded carefully, and you get updates while your belongings travel to your new home.

What's Included

  • A move plan with pickup and delivery dates
  • Full furniture wrapping and padding
  • Careful loading and secure tie-downs
  • Updates while your things are on the road
  • Unloading and furniture placement
  • Optional packing and short-term storage

Tips Before Your Move

Book early

Long-distance moves fill up fast in summer. Contact us 4 to 8 weeks ahead if you can.

Make a list

A simple room-by-room list of large items helps us plan the right truck and crew.

Keep valuables with you

Carry jewelry, important papers, and medicine yourself.

Long-Distance Moving Questions

How far ahead should I book a long-distance move?

Four to eight weeks ahead is best, especially in summer. We can sometimes handle moves on shorter notice, so call to check.

How is a long-distance move priced?

Long-distance prices depend on distance, how much you're moving, packing needs, and access at both homes. We give you a free quote after going over your move.

Can you store my things if my new home isn't ready?

Yes. We offer storage between your pickup and delivery dates.
